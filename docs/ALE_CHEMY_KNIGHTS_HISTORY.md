# The Ale-Chemy Knights — Recovered History

Last synthesized: 2026-09-26

## Canon status

The Ale-Chemy Knights are a distinct faction. The user identifies **Tobias, Farkur, and Ryvyt as its main members**. The supplied faction synthesis explicitly identifies **Farkur and Ryvyt as founders**; Tobias is a main member and major builder, but is not promoted to founder without further evidence.

The best supported institutional description is a **self-funded adventuring cooperative and mutual-aid institution**. The Knights connect specialized labor, scouting, crafting, equipment, property, transport, information, newcomer support, public works, and reconstruction. They are not established as Prima’s government, a standing army, a conventional charity, or merely a private shop.

Their call is:

> “One for all…” — “And all for one!”

## Main members

### Farkur S. Gemblenagin

Farkur is a founder, builder, organizer, and principal institutional architect. His central pattern is to build something larger than himself: finish work, honor obligations, connect different capabilities, fund durable systems, and improve the part of the world within reach even when nothing lasts forever.

Farkur’s companions include **Green Bean**, a winged emerald snake, and **Fangs**, a mechanical or steel companion. Their coordination reflects his broader organizational instinct.

### Ryvyt

Ryvyt is a founder, crafter, researcher, and early operational specialist. Farkur explicitly calls Ryvyt “our crafter” when directing specialized work beyond his own expertise. Ryvyt borrows from shared inventory for jobs and asks that project requests be left behind for completion.

Ryvyt’s research interests include Delerium. This belongs to Ryvyt’s personal record and is not automatically faction policy.

### Tobias

Tobias is a main member, builder, newcomer guide, and practical support figure. Tobias provides one of the strongest demonstrations that the Knights’ proposed welcome system became real: safety, food, orientation, a map, lodging and employment information, escort, and personal financial help for the newcomer Fredrick.

Tobias describes the Knights as helping adventurers become more independent rather than dependent upon one settlement. By 8 December 2025, Tobias is described as missing again. The source does not recover the earlier absence or establish the later outcome.

## Important associates and operational figures

- **Ruben** — scout, prospector, resource specialist, and operational representative. He locates and records resources, explains the economic system, and recognizes the organization as a legacy and family business.
- **Anky** — inner-circle associate or founder-circle figure, religious intermediary, and the Knights’ “Saint.” Exact formal status remains imprecise.
- **Pappy** — close social and material contributor. Association alone does not establish formal membership.
- **Fenwick** — connected to Farkur’s early planning and proposed communication network.
- **Severina** — materially connected through resurrection history, Pristinia public works, and economic interactions; not established as a defining leader.
- **Green Bean and Fangs** — Farkur’s companions and part of his practical working system.

## 17 November 2024 — Friendly Guides and Grub

At the Common Man, Farkur proposes **Farkur’s Friendly Guides and Grub**, a Gate-side caravan service offering newcomers maps, food, ale, local knowledge or curiosities, and eventual paid carriage transport. He speaks openly about profit and attracting customers. Pappy warns that the Gate and its surroundings are spiritually significant.

This is a clear precursor to the Knights’ later newcomer-support system. It does not prove the Ale-Chemy Knights formally existed at that date.

## 15 December 2024 — Tobias answers an ordinary shortage

At the Common Man, Tobias orders ale and volunteers when failed harvest support and dwindling winter stores produce a community hunt. Herb limits the hunt to protect the following year’s herd.

The scene establishes practical civic response. It does not by itself date the faction’s foundation.

## January–early 2025 — Ciderwood Sanctum and the welcome system

At **Ciderwood Sanctum on Forevermoore**, the very small auxiliary island off Eovar’s coast established by the user on 10 October 2026, Farkur develops the Gate-service proposal into a wider institution: a proper welcome, orientation, transportation, lore, warm drinks, contacts, records of arrivals and skills, communication, and emergency response. Known or strongly supported Sanctum features include animal space, a forge, new construction, an orchard, and nearby material clearing. Its complete layout and formal settlement status remain unknown.

Tobias later performs the system with Fredrick, turning the plan into practice.

## 25 July 2025 — Shop, loans, crafting, and property

By July, the Knights maintain an inventory and equipment pool. Ryvyt requests the **Hazewind Cape**, **Puzzler’s Wit ring**, and a Bag of Holding for a job; Farkur marks them as loaned. Farkur directs specialized crafting toward Ryvyt.

The organization is financially constrained: customers lack money, Farkur’s purse is light, and work is scarce. Even so, the Knights vote to purchase a tavern in **Eovar** while Farkur repairs it. The collective vote prevents that asset from being treated automatically as Farkur’s private property.

## Late 2025 — Information, resources, and public works

The Knights formalize exploration through maps, field markings, a **Cartographer’s Map Case**, and an **Elder Cartography Glossary**. Ruben’s principle is “Flight now, haul later”: locate and preserve the route first, then let the wider network extract and transform the resource.

Organizational funds and materials support Pristinia wall upgrades and at least **two towers**. New arrivals can be paid for supervised wall construction, with minimum-work expectations and a prohibition on unsafe solo labor.

## 8 December 2025 — The mature economic system

Ruben explains an integrated operation that:

- purchases and sells raw materials;
- pays skilled labor;
- gathers specialized components;
- crafts, lends, rents, finances, and sells equipment;
- purchases existing goods for resale;
- maintains pooled organizational money;
- invests in businesses and property;
- funds defenses and public work;
- operates ships and supply chains;
- prepares for reconstruction and war logistics.

Ruben describes magical equipment rented for roughly ten percent of cost per mission, with ownership after a twelfth mission. **Gregory Greenleaf challenges the ethics of the effective financing cost.** The record does not declare either interpretation objectively correct.

By this point the Knights report two ships carrying goods from elsewhere and discuss moving a larger troupe through the Convergence toward Gael. Tobias is described as missing again.

## 14 February 2026 — Reputation becomes infrastructure

Draven Montauk directs the newcomer Iron Saint Valorum to the Ale-Chemy Knights as the best available information source. He gives Valorum a handbell that appears silent but can, according to Draven, be heard by the Knights.

The bell’s maker, range, number, magical mechanism, and listening rules remain unknown. Draven identifies Farkur and Ryvyt as founders and says they are away on an important job.

## 2026 — Gael reconstruction and the open-air market

By 19 September 2026, the Knights have established an open-air market in Gael with areas divided by skill set. Marius remembers Gael as having almost no economy. His comparison remains attributed; the market itself is confirmed.

The market shows that at least part of the organization’s planned reconstruction beyond Prima became durable civic history.

## The disappearance

By the same September record, **Farkur and other Knights have vanished**. The source does not identify a cause, captor, destination, mission, or outcome. Ryvyt reportedly takes the disappearance hard. The statement establishes serious personal consequence without preserving Ryvyt’s exact words or response.

Tobias’s status at this later point remains unclear. The earlier record of repeated absence shows that dependence on key specialists is one of the faction’s vulnerabilities.

## Holdings and tools

Known or reported assets include:

- an Eovar tavern property approved for purchase;
- two ships;
- Pristinia wall and tower investment;

- maps and resource-marking systems;
- the Gael open-air market.

Exact ownership and current status must be updated as later evidence establishes changes.

## World role

The Knights’ strongest world functions are:

1. **Newcomer integration** — preventing new arrivals from being abandoned without orientation, safety, work, or transport.
2. **Economic coordination** — connecting scouts, hunters, crafters, smiths, builders, merchants, customers, and projects.
3. **Public infrastructure** — converting pooled wealth and labor into walls, towers, transport, property, and reconstruction.
4. **Knowledge and logistics** — preserving maps, resources, routes, inventory, communication, and supply chains.
5. **Reconstruction** — applying the organization’s model beyond Prima, most clearly in Gael.

These functions create value and influence. The faction should not be presented as universally trusted. Financing terms, pooled wealth, property, selectivity, vertical integration, and public works can be understood as useful institution-building or as concentrated soft power depending upon the observer.
