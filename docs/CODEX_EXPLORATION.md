# Exploring the Codex — 6 October 2026

Requested additions: character art/quotes, connected location histories, typed search filters, expanded relationship maps, and a new-reader path.

## Character collections

Character pages gather their supplied portrait, associated Ececilia artwork, and attributed public gallery quotes in an Art & words section. Existing portrait captions and Ececilia credit are preserved. The six community pieces route to Gartina, Elenia, Magnus, Carmen/Paco, Scribonia, and the Seekers/Vessalia. Existing quote recordings use the same in-box playback controls in these collections. A piece’s title does not establish a biographical event.

## Connected histories

Both Places and Locations categories now receive place chronologies. Location pages show relevant existing map links, people explicitly named in recovered events, parent places, and indexed sublocations. Names resolve through directory names, aliases, or a unique expanded name; unresolved collective names do not create invented characters. Event dialogs link to their people and locations. Public visibility checks apply to each linked record and timeline event.

## Search

All, Characters, Locations, Events, and Quotes filters narrow the existing global search, before deduplication. Article results classify by their location category or indexed character identity. Quote search and direct navigation remain intact. Clearing a selected result restores All for the next query.

## Relationship maps

Five additional maps: Vaemyr, Saray, Swift Foot, Huoth, and Witch Queen Ephraith. New data is in character-map-expansion.js, synthesized from linked public character histories and supplied dossiers. Selected connections include dated or explicitly undated turning points; source limits remain visible. Filters are limited to categories represented in that map. Existing character and faction maps remain in place.

## Reading path

Start Here is linked from the homepage, sidebar, and Ethos. Six optional steps introduce the Ethos, atlas, people, factions, a connected location history, and quotes/community artwork. Readers manually mark steps as read. Only their checked step IDs are saved locally on the device; no account or tracking service is added.
