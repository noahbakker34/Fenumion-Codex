# Broader editorial refinement · 24 October 2026

The owner’s “Continue” after the completed eight-entry pilot authorizes Phase 2 of the supplied editorial brief. This is a targeted prose pass; the pilot’s requirement to wait for approval has been fulfilled.

## Scope and method

Catalogue-wide screening considered public narrative paragraphs, longer entries’ descriptions and profile summaries. Rhetorical patterns were prompts for individual review, not automatic replacement rules. The first scan found 156 candidate paragraphs in 96 records; a second scan found 19 further paragraph/description candidates. Many remain appropriate: mechanics, evidence limits, doctrine, attributed dialogue, genuine contrasts and effective prose are left alone. This is not a claim to reread every source message or every sentence of the archive.

The final change touches 61 existing records: 104 individual paragraphs, 14 short descriptions, ten matching brief summaries and six profile fields (including a matching description copy). No records or history sections are added or removed. The selected bodies retain over 99% of their combined word count; shortening is incidental, not the objective. All eight pilot entries remain exactly as published.

Original quest and compatible RP samples were consulted alongside the histories. Quest outcomes outrank conflicting RP/syntheses; character speech remains attributed. Raw transcripts, search extracts and temporary editing plans stay outside Git and the public site.
