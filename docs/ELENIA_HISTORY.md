# Elenia — Detailed Character Profile and History

Last synthesized: 2026-09-23

## Canon status

This dossier separates established events, attributed recollections, interpretation, and unresolved chronology. Elenia’s public record is unusually rich, but several important episodes survive only through later retellings or secondary synthesis rather than a complete primary battle log.

## Identity at a glance

- **Name:** Elenia
- **Known role:** Cleric; bearer of light; later publicly remembered as the Savior of Pristinia
- **Divine relationship:** Cala is her earliest named divine patron; Mya becomes a major later relationship
- **Close relationships:** Nienna, Adelia, Mya, Quake, Eugene, Gartina, Aria, Magnus, Nymera
- **Core trajectory:** wonder → curiosity → experience → meaning → practice → responsibility
- **Central strength:** converts prior experience and loss into protection for someone else
- **Central danger:** capability becoming permission to decide for others
- **Primary question:** Can Elenia become a caretaker without becoming an owner?

## Core character

Elenia does not begin with certainty. Her earliest surviving scenes show someone willing to follow wonder while admitting that she may be mistaken. That uncertainty becomes important as her power grows. The later Elenia can break siege towers, restore the dead, alter a body through divine magic, open a Gate, and participate in reshaping a mountain. The early Elenia, however, follows flowers through a forest and says that perhaps she was merely hopeful.

Her defining pattern is not passive goodness. She acts, often quickly and with overwhelming force. What distinguishes her strongest choices is that experience becomes practice: a spell that once saved her and Nienna is later placed on Quake; Nienna’s death becomes a reason to preserve attachment rather than reject it; Mya’s hope becomes a method of acting into uncertainty; power is repeatedly directed toward helping another person stand, flee, return, learn, or choose.

The same pattern contains her danger. Elenia can move from “I can help” to “I know what must be done” faster than other people can meaningfully consent. Her history therefore cannot be flattened into “good goddess,” “powerful healer,” or “the strongest hero.” The important question is what happens when a fundamentally helpful person becomes capable of deciding almost everything.

## Chronological history

### April 2024 — Wonder before settled faith

Elenia’s earliest clear divine scene takes place around a possible call from Cala. She knows Cala only loosely as a lady of light. She follows the possibility of a call through a forest, notices flowers and beauty, experiments with the environment, and admits uncertainty:

> “I think Cala was calling me … but maybe I was just hopeful.”

At the altar she offers to serve and to become an instrument carrying light into dark places. Her early loop is:

`wonder → curiosity → experience → meaning → action`

This is not yet mature faith. It is openness paired with movement. Later growth complicates the language of becoming an instrument because Elenia learns to judge divine authority rather than treating obedience as virtue by itself.

### June 2024 — Mya, wonder, and the first living foothold

Elenia meets Mya, the divine caretaker associated with Hope. Mya tells her to remain a child of wonder and to be a light where darkness is strongest. Elenia answers with action rather than doctrinal agreement: she casts Light.

The broader Gael record places Elenia and Adelia among the people who establish or prove an early living foothold in land that Death had defeated. The exact division of labor and date of every restoration act remain unresolved. The archive should not assign the creation of Hope to one hero. The defensible communal sequence is:

1. Gael is encountered as a land defeated by Death.
2. Elenia and Adelia help establish or prove a living foothold.
3. Gartina begins practical ecological restoration.
4. Nienna, Dale, and others provide labor, resources, defense, and sacrifice.
5. Hope survives and later grows.

### 8 July 2024 — Wrath’s offer after Dale’s death

Wrath appears beside grief and offers a path through Death after killing Dale. Quake refuses. Elenia also refuses to accept Wrath’s premise and asks Cala for another way. A diamond makes restoration possible.

The scene establishes a pattern that remains central: Elenia is willing to seek divine aid without accepting that the first powerful being to offer an answer owns the choice.

### 18 July 2024 — Experience becomes Death Ward

Before another confrontation with Wrath, Elenia casts Death Ward on Quake because the magic had previously saved her and Nienna:

> “This saved Nienna and I.”

The grammar is more important than perfect wording. Elenia does not merely remember an earlier rescue. She turns it into a later protection. Nienna is already becoming part of Elenia’s future conduct before Nienna’s final sacrifice.

Elenia also attacks the useful portal rather than remaining trapped in Wrath’s argument. Spiritual openness does not prevent tactical judgment.

### October 2024 — Acting under Death and Delerium pressure

The surviving chronology records a staff-splinter or broken Eldrin-staff event connected to Delerium, Wrath, and Death. Elenia continues to act despite the danger. The precise scene mechanics require a primary-source pass, but the event belongs to the development of action under cosmic pressure rather than belief as an identity label.

### 21 November 2024 — Wrath cleaves Elenia

During the assault on Wrath, Arjahn offers himself, Wrath cuts Elenia, and Saray ultimately lands the final blow. A later community retrospective remembers Gartina calling Elenia back after Wrath cleaved her in two.

The exact resurrection or recovery mechanics should be retrieved before being stated more precisely. What is established is that Elenia suffered catastrophic harm in the confrontation and returned to the continuing record.

### Late 2024 to February 2025 — Gael continues without her

Before 24 February 2025, Adelia leaves Gael with a small group that definitely includes Elenia. The destination is not established in the surviving regional account. Gartina remains behind because refugees, Shadowangel Forest, and Gael’s unfinished work still require attention.

This departure separates two forms of caretaking. Elenia continues into wider divine and political conflict; Gartina stays with the ordinary labor required after miraculous restoration.

### 2025 — The Rahu siege and Savior of Pristinia

During the Rahu siege, Elenia breaks the siege towers. Chronicler material publicly frames her as the **Savior of Pristinia**.

This is a major change in scale and reputation. The uncertain forest pilgrim has become a figure whose intervention can alter a siege and whose name can be made into public memory. The title is evidence of reputation, not proof that she alone saved the settlement.

### 2025 — Nienna, Aria, and conditional faith

Nienna remains one of the central causal relationships in Elenia’s history. Elenia describes her as a sister and uses the loss to understand Aria rather than simply to condemn attachment. Aria argues that love creates future weakness because loved people cannot always be protected. Elenia’s history answers that vulnerability does not make love a mistake.

In a private bath conversation with Aria, Elenia asks about siblings and speaks from her relationship with Nienna. The scene also intersects with an unresolved contradiction in Aria’s history: Aria says she has no siblings despite an earlier account involving an older brother.

Elenia’s faith has matured by this point. She can trust Mya while judging Talan, Nuru, Aionia, and the divine order independently. She does not need the gods to be flawless before acting with them, and she does not treat divine power as proof of moral correctness.

One later formulation captures the distinction:

> “Less to rekindle my faith, more to kindle any faith in the Zarathians.”

### 6 December 2025 — Magnus and the limit of restraint

After a divine battle in which Elenia says she resurrected roughly one hundred people, Magnus challenges her or tries to turn others against her in a fight-to-the-death context described as a duel over who was right.

Elenia wins but does not kill him. Instead, she uses divine magic to remove or erase his face.

> “It was a duel to see who was right, but I didn’t want to kill him.”

The act is restraint without innocence. Refusing execution does not make bodily alteration gentle, and preserving a life does not automatically grant permission to transform it. The event contributes to Elenia’s exile or banishment from Fein Uaill in the detailed Magnus profile and current public chronology.

One derivative master-timeline summary instead says Magnus was banished. Because the detailed profile explicitly connects the wider reaction to Elenia’s exile, the Codex currently favors Elenia as the banished party while keeping the discrepancy open until the primary battle and judgment records are recovered.

### 15 January 2026 — Ink for Eugene

Elenia obtains ink for Eugene’s spellbook. This is a small event by scale and a major one by character logic. Someone capable of resurrection, siege-breaking, and divine violence invests materially in another person’s ability to learn.

The juxtaposition matters: Elenia recounts resurrection, exile, and the Magnus confrontation while carrying out an errand for a friend. Cosmic authority does not remove her from ordinary care.

### 2026 — Brianna’s sanctification

After Magnus forces Delerium into Brianna, Elenia calls upon Mya. Hope cleanses or sanctifies the crystal rather than simply removing it. The result transforms the residue of violation without pretending the violation never occurred.

This episode is one of the clearest examples of Elenia and Mya acting through prior harm rather than around it. The exact date and mechanical division among Elenia, Mya, Adelia, Farkur, and the others present should be recovered before further precision.

### 2026 — The Miracle of Fenumion

Elenia dies and is returned through Reincarnate as a satyr. The mechanical result was an unfudged one-in-250 roll. Before the result, the party had accepted that she might return changed.

The event functions like grace because it remained contingent. It should not be canonized as direct intervention by Ada unless later lore explicitly establishes that claim. Its force depends on the fact that nobody guaranteed the result and the players had already accepted loss and transformation.

The exact date, cause of Elenia’s death, and relationship between this event and the surrounding 2026 chronology remain unresolved in the current synthesis.

### August 2026 — A mountain unmade

Elenia joins Scribonia, Eugene, Arjahn, and others in a mountain-scale intervention. Storm, Earthquake, fissures, avalanche, floodwater, lava, boulders, and a vast column of steam and smoke alter the landscape.

The surviving narration describes the mountain clawing at its own face and visibility disappearing into “biblical destruction.” Before acting, Elenia prays that unknown people in the storm’s path be protected or removed. During retreat she watches for other people fleeing. She asks where the boundary lies, accepts “no,” and uses Gate to send Nymera home.

The scene neither absolves nor simply condemns her. It demonstrates that her power has reached a scale at which good intentions can reshape the world. Her attention still returns to individuals and limits, but the radius of what she can destroy has become enormous.

## Power development

Elenia’s power should be read as accumulated responsibility, not as a disconnected escalation of abilities.

1. **Wonder and Light:** follows an uncertain call and responds to Mya by making light.
2. **Protective memory:** uses Death Ward because earlier protection worked.
3. **Alternative to Death:** refuses Wrath’s offered solution and seeks another means of restoration.
4. **Survival of catastrophic harm:** returns after Wrath cleaves her.
5. **Regional intervention:** helps establish living Gael and later destroys siege towers at Pristinia.
6. **Mass restoration:** reports resurrecting roughly one hundred people after a god battle.
7. **Transformative judgment:** removes Magnus’s face rather than killing him.
8. **Healing through history:** calls Mya to sanctify what was done to Brianna.
9. **Contingent return:** comes back through an extraordinary Reincarnate result.
10. **World-shaping force:** participates in unmaking a mountain and opens a Gate for Nymera.

The pattern is coherent because each increase creates a larger test of consent, restraint, and ownership.

## Relationships

### Cala

Cala is Elenia’s earliest named divine orientation. Elenia moves from instrument language toward conditional service. Cala remains associated with light and heroic urgency, but Elenia’s faith becomes capable of judging divine failure.

### Mya

Mya turns hope from an emotion into enacted possibility. Their relationship joins wonder, costly restoration, Brianna’s sanctification, and the attempt to kindle faith among Zarathians. Elenia trusts Mya without making that trust a blanket endorsement of all gods.

### Nienna

Nienna is sisterhood, grief, and causal memory. Earlier protection of Elenia and Nienna changes how Elenia protects Quake. Nienna’s later sacrifice influences how Elenia understands Aria, love, and Hope. Death does not remove Nienna from Elenia’s decision-making.

### Adelia

Elenia and Adelia help establish living Gael and later leave the region together. Both become examples of extraordinary power tied to caretaking, but they should not be collapsed into one philosophy. Their diverging paths after Gael remain incompletely documented.

### Quake

Quake’s earlier aid becomes the reason Elenia places Death Ward on him. Their relationship is one of the cleanest examples of care returning through time.

### Aria

Aria gives Elenia a philosophical opponent rather than only an enemy. Nienna allows Elenia to meet Pride’s argument about attachment with lived counterevidence. Elenia can understand Aria’s fear without treating fear as absolution.

### Magnus

Magnus tests the boundary between restraint and bodily domination. Elenia refuses to kill him but imposes a terrifying alternative. The duel and its political consequences expose the danger in believing that mercy automatically legitimizes whatever replaces death.

### Eugene

The ink errand shows Elenia investing in knowledge carried by someone who doubts his own worth. It keeps her history grounded in material support rather than divine spectacle alone.

### Gartina

Their histories intersect in Gael’s restoration and the assault on Wrath. A later retrospective remembers Gartina helping call Elenia back after catastrophic injury. Their paths also embody complementary forms of care: Elenia creates openings at miraculous scale; Gartina stays to make survival durable.

### Nymera

Nymera is another follower of Cala who questions why devotees reach for violence when protection may not require it. Elenia’s use of Gate to send her home preserves Nymera’s ability to leave rather than making shared faith a claim on her continued participation.

## Moral and thematic interpretation

### Caretaking is not ownership

Elenia’s best acts preserve another person’s ability to stand, learn, return, flee, or choose. Her worst danger is assuming that her willingness to bear a cost authorizes her to choose the form of someone else’s salvation.

### Power does not create permission

The face-wipe is the sharpest example. She may have rejected death, but overwhelming capability still allowed her to impose a permanent bodily judgment. The scene must remain morally difficult.

### History becomes practice

Elenia remembers functionally. Death Ward, Nienna, Mya, and prior loss become future conduct. This is why her arc feels cumulative rather than episodic.

### Wonder survives knowledge

Mature Elenia knows more about divine failure than early Elenia and still remains capable of hope. Wonder is no longer innocence. It is a deliberate openness that survives disillusionment.
