## Related-history correction

The runtime formerly treated every tag, including companions’ names, as an identity when matching chronicle events. That caused unrelated events to appear on biographies, including a risk of joining the two Auroras through a shared tag. Matching now uses the record title, explicit identity aliases and explicit event-to-record links for places and factions. The caravan event’s ambiguous Aurora participant label is expanded to Aurora Luminaria, confirmed by the quest’s attributed posts (for example 4101 and 4104). No generic Aurora alias joins the two biographies. Groups and multi-site journeys receive explicit relevant links rather than inheriting all their members’ histories.
