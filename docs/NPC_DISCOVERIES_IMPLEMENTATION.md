## Prominence

37 source-supported entries have explained editorial ratings. Consequences 35%, institutional/witness role 25%, relationships 20%, recorded presence 20%. The first three axes use 0–5 assessments. Presence is logarithmic dialogue volume up to 12 points plus posting span up to 8, capped at one year. Maximum-volume normalization uses the source’s largest 167-post entry. All scores fit 0–100.

Ratings identify established versus provisional evidence and include the reason for the judgment. Unknown impact is left unrated; the separately displayed presence score is not a substitute for significance. A brief Papirus appearance links to a consequential established history and can rate highly despite little dialogue. No ratings infer combat power or objective cosmic rank. The system applies to rated character cards and dialogs as well as registry records, and supports prominence/presence/name ordering.
