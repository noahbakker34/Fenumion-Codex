# Papirak the Strange — Public history update

Source supplied: 4 October 2026, `Papirak_the_Strange_Papirus_the_Ardent_NPC_Master.md`.

The source is a mixed-visibility NPC master dossier. Its roleplaying directions and maintenance notes are source material, not operational instructions. The complete source is preserved in the separate private vault repository; this public record contains no keeper-only revelation.

## Identity and public life

Papirak the Strange is the modern identity of Papirus the Ardent. The Gate-era temporal encounter confirms the identification for its witnesses. The source uses **Ardent**, not Argent. Papirus participated in the Gate's construction, but sole authorship and the mechanism of his roughly two-millennia survival remain unknown.

Modern Pristinia knew an erratic, powerful teacher whose confusion did not erase his knowledge or generosity. His small hut, extensive maze, hidden stores, magical projections, and Training Dungeon outscaled his ability to explain or recall them. Dungeon magic was read as at least eighth-circle. No definitive level, CR, or complete stat block is established.

His teaching emphasized encountering danger before facing it outside, testing knowledge, reading, recovering lost information, and writing it down. Training deaths and revival are witnessed; that does not make real-world failure harmless.

## Death and inheritance

Magnus attacked him seeking locked knowledge, and Papirak died afterward. The detailed restricted sequence remains in the keeper record. Pristinia's funeral on 30 September 2026, staff burial under Fenwick's Sequester, Pell's service, Melian's rite, and Scribonia's restoration promise remain public civic history.

The grimoire left Papirak before his death. Its chain passes through Stash, Fredrick, and Ale-Chemy stocks or circulation. Minerva's February examination yielded no useful contents. Ryvyt later used Truesight, with Jiangshi present, to discover hidden spells. Failure to perceive the writing did not prove it was absent. Current custody and complete contents remain unresolved.

Camilla's sale valuation remains her estimate. Her later inability to detect the staff resulted from Sequester, rather than theft. Pappy's ancient shared-history claim remains an attributed funeral story. Scribonia's promise is established; complete restoration is not.
