## Editorial selection

The 222 gallery candidates are proposals, not canon decisions. All eight contact sheets and the final Saelihn image were visually reviewed. A local, read-only metadata scan indexes 428,263 unique archived messages and finds 5,675 messages under the candidate labels; this is not a claim to have read the entire archive. Selected original messages and quest context were checked for the resulting histories. No raw transcript, controller roster, private conversation or entire gallery is copied into Git.

The final selection promotes 43 existing compact discovery records into named NPC profiles, rather than creating duplicate people. Their acts establish a concrete role in a quest: guidance, teaching, representation, investigation, rescue, diplomacy or a substantive confrontation. Ahuic’s festival sparring appearance was withheld on the same principle as the other thinly characterized training labels. Generic guards, workers, numbered pirates, unresolved duplicates, brief gag appearances and private-only identity material remain unpromoted. Quagmire, Michael, the default Discord icon and NVR-MOAR receive no portrait installation. The previously imported census records retain their stable archive routes; this update does not recreate them as NPC-directory entries.

A comic name or familiar image does not erase a quest role. Osu explicitly calls Yoshida a lead architect and explains that Shogun is a joke, so the profile establishes craftsmanship and diplomatic conversation, not military command. The Fog Courts teachers are retained for their documented work in the apprenticeship trials. Vys is not merged into Vysaeth; Mirae is not merged into Míriel; Crimson Joe’s local John introduction is not merged into the existing John player character. The mermaid labeled Nila retains her explicit name uncertainty. Political nominations, religious accusations, proposed expeditions and explanations of spirits remain attributed and bounded.

Saelihn was already a player-directory character. Her same entry now leads to a personal profile supported by the Common Man reconstruction and Viota expedition, preserving the shared campaign history. There are still 99 player-directory entries. The 43 NPC additions bring the NPC directory to 151; island assignments are made only where supported, with the rest staying unplaced. No prominence ratings are invented or increased. The original census counts/spans are labeled as original census metadata, rather than presented as the complete newly checked record.
