# Separate custom Fenumion and background playlists

13 November 2026. Owner asks to organize the remaining songs into playlists and keep Suno tracks in their own playlists because they are custom Fenumion music.

The player’s Playlist selector has two native option groups: **Custom Fenumion · Suno** and **Background soundtrack**. The former mixed All styles queue is removed. All Fenumion songs contains only the forty custom recordings; All background music contains only the 139 existing background tracks. Playlist membership is based on the existing separate source catalogues, not a guessed instrument, filename extension or mood.

Every custom recording has exactly one thematic home:

| I Am | 14 | All thirteen I Am songs plus Saint Anky |

These are editorial listening groups, not new canon or claims that the audio independently establishes an event, place or character fact. Prima membership retains the generation-prompt evidence and its limits from the preceding update. No custom track appears in any background playlist or vice versa.

Recommendations are now limited to the selected playlist, including character suggestions within I Am. Choosing a suggestion keeps that playlist. Next, previous, completion and repeat use that same membership. Counts identify Custom Fenumion or Background music rather than suggesting a mixed 179-track queue. A saved custom track from the old All styles list migrates to All Fenumion songs; a saved background selection stays in the background collection. Other valid saved playlists/recordings persist, and incompatible saved pairs recover to a valid track in their selected playlist.
