# The Vanguard — recovered history

Last synthesized: 2026-10-12

## Reconciled status

The earlier provisional entry was based on a single May 2026 battlefield reference. Selected primary Gael scenes now establish named Vanguard self-identification in 2024–2025. These records supersede the claim that Arjahn was its only named member or that May 2026 was its first recovered appearance. They do not settle the entire organization’s charter, founding, command, roster or fate.

## Early company testimony

- 6 July 2024 UTC: Gartina says “we in the Vanguard” have witnessed sacrifice and consequence (`1259231485592998054`, Tower `1250334670957051958`).
- 24 October 2024 UTC: Scribonia says he was part of the Vanguard while describing losses he has borne (`1299046910069440613`, Plains `1250445552424980590`).
- 24 February 2025 UTC: Gartina explicitly identifies herself as a member while choosing to remain for refugees and new forest work, then describes her duty to the wider fight (`1343643791235616788`, `1343654460068728875`, Tower).

These are character accounts of belonging and duty. No complete formal roster, command hierarchy, charter or founding date is inferred from them.

## The May 2026 report remains distinct

The Babel-Ashur source describes Arjahn as part of a losing vanguard on 5 May 2026. Lowercase battlefield wording alone does not establish whether it names the earlier company, a forward formation or another body. Arjahn’s earlier rescue/protective history remains in his character dossier; it does not retrospectively prove membership at every earlier scene. July’s cave survivors and Knights’ hunt are not automatically identified with this formation.

## Later collaboration

26 July 2026 UTC: Lady Severina says collaboration between Veilguard and Vanguard is the goal (`1530769622276509799`, Tower). This is a proposal, not a completed merger or alliance charter. The Veilguard’s separately recovered new-vanguard formation and leadership history remains intact.

## Open questions

Founding and original command, full membership over time, institutional continuity between the early company and May battlefield formation, later survival or absences, and the final form of any successor cooperation.
